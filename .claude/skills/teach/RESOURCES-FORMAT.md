# RESOURCES.md Format

`RESOURCES.md` is the curated set of trusted sources for the topic. Lesson knowledge comes from here, and wisdom from the communities listed here.

```md
# {Topic} Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _The Science and Practice of Strength Training_ by Zatsiorsky & Kraemer](https://example.com)
  Foundational text on programming and adaptation. Use for: periodisation, recovery, intensity zones.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/weightroom](https://reddit.com/r/weightroom)
  High-signal subreddit, moderated against bro-science. Use for: programme critique, plateaus.

## Gaps

- {An area the mission needs that no good resource covers yet}
```

- High-trust only: primary sources, recognised experts, peer-reviewed work, well-moderated communities. Leave out marketing dressed as education.
- Annotate every entry with what it covers and when to reach for it.
- Keep `## Gaps` current; it drives the next search.
- Remove a resource that proved wrong, shallow or off-mission.
- If the user opted out of communities, note it here so later sessions stop proposing them.
