## Self-Test Checklist

Before presenting a draft to the user, verify:

- [ ] **Specificity:** Contains at least one named company, specific number, or concrete example (not abstract "enterprises" or "at scale")
- [ ] **Human-sound markers:** Contains at least one L1 interference pattern, sentence fragment, or non-standard construction
- [ ] **No AI-smell:** Zero instances of banned words/phrases/structures
- [ ] **Hook quality:** Opening line works before the fold — would you click "see more"?
- [ ] **Humor check:** If the post is >100 words, does it contain at least one moment of wit, irony, or personality? (Not forced — skip if the topic demands seriousness)
- [ ] **Persuasion technique:** Uses at least one adopted technique (Killer Quote, escalation, contrast, P.S. reveal)
- [ ] **Length appropriate:** Matches the template's target length for this content type
- [ ] **Voice coherence:** Reads as one person, not a patchwork of three different voices
- [ ] **Not over-polished:** Has enough rough edges to feel human — perfect parallel structures and flowing academic prose are red flags
