- engineering-loop: `land.batch_wait_seconds` (default 0: no wait) makes `land`, holding the lock, wait up to that long before it gates while another open PR into the same base is held only by its required checks still running; each such PR that turns landable joins the batch and shares its one gate. The wait and who joined are printed and shown on the `Landed` comment's `Batch:` line; `land --only` never waits (#1032).
