- engineering-loop: risk is a rating and gatekeeper.md's new `levels` section says what each level needs. `low`, `high` and `critical` each take `spec` (`policy` or `owner`) and `merge` (`gates`, `go` or `owner`), so a project can land high risk on the gates, or hold every merge for a go, without changing what rates high. The defaults reproduce the previous rule. `risk.merge_paths` is renamed `risk.critical_paths`: the old name is read for one release with a `deprecated:` line, and setting both is refused. `check merge` and `land` print the level and the merge action they applied on stderr (#1154).
