- engineering-loop: `land --wait` takes a free landing lock in waiting order. Each waiting land keeps a ref `loop-waiting/<base>/<id>` stamped at every poll and holding when it began to wait; a land that finds the lock free leaves it to a longer-waiting land whose stamp is under 7 minutes old, so a waiter that died does not hold the queue. The ref is deleted when the land takes the lock or stops waiting. A land without `--wait` ignores the order. (#1216)
