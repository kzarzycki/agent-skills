- engineering-loop: `land --wait` takes a free landing lock in waiting order. Each waiting land keeps a branch `loop-waiting/<base>/<id>`: a new commit on it every poll (a fast-forward, so a cloud session can push it), holding when the land began to wait, and a `done` commit when it stops. A land that finds the lock free leaves it to a longer-waiting land stamped under 10 minutes ago, so a waiter that died does not hold the queue. A land keeps its place while it takes the lock and lands nothing, and across the rerun after the base moves; a SIGTERM or SIGHUP ends the wait cleanly. A land without `--wait` ignores the order.
