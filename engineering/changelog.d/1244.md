- orchestrating-agents: `start` no longer abandons a session whose brief is still pending, or whose history has moved past `resource_event`, when the confirm window ends: it polls for `HTTP_SECONDS` more and keeps the session if the brief lands, so a slow server's brief is not sent again to a `-r2` session; a start with an empty history and nothing pending is abandoned as before, and a failed start exits 1 through `main` (#1244).
