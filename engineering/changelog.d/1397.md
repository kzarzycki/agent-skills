- A verifier's `Risk:` is now the highest `Risk:` of its passes since the last pass that reviewed the whole change: its first pass, or a later pass whose closing `Scope: full` line says so (exactly `full`; `verdict` now posts the line and `report --json` has a `scope` key). A `low` on a delta (a rebase, a merge, a hand change) no longer drops `risk:high` or the owner's go, and a disputed `high` is re-judged only by a `Scope: full` pass; `judged` reads it for `check merge`, `land`, `check queue` and `unflag` (#1397).
