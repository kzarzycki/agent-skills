- `acceptance.py` parses a sh or bash check before running it, with its shebang's shell and flags plus `-n` (and `-O extglob` for bash), and reports one that does not parse as `FAIL ... does not parse`. Under `set -e`, bash 3.2 (macOS's /bin/bash) runs the EXIT trap on a syntax error and exits with the trap's status, so a broken check could pass (#1545).
