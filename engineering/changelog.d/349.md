- engineering-loop: `approvals.py check merge` waits for the checks `land` reads (the base's required checks, or with none every check on the head) instead of only the aggregate `check`, so the two never disagree on one head; it never reads the `loop:approvals` status it posts nor the job it runs in, which would make it wait on itself, while every other job of its workflow counts, running or not. The approvals workflow passes that job as `APPROVALS_CHECK_RUN_ID: ${{ job.check_run_id }}` in its env; in Actions without it, `check merge` holds with a line naming the variable (#349).
