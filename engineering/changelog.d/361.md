- engineering-loop: a project may land through a batching merge queue of its own. `approvals.py check queue <pr> --head <sha>` says whether a head may enter it (every merge proof but the CI wait, the head's own check passed, not high risk, no `approved:merge`); `approvals.py batch` posts the queue's proof on each PR it merged; `check merge` accepts that proof in place of the CI wait once git shows the head merged unchanged, with no conflict resolution, into a batch whose full gate passed (#361).
