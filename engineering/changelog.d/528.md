- A `run` that ends without a report, failed or stopped by SIGTERM, SIGINT or SIGHUP, writes `FAILED: <code>: <message>` to its report path (the child's state and `last_task_error` code, else the launcher's own), never over a report the child wrote, and `wait`, `send`, `start` and the supervisor never take that line as a report; `lead.md`'s wait rule waits on a child's status or a run's pid rather than its report file, watches for a run gone without an outcome, rechecks its runs on every wake, treats a child with no turn item as dead, stops a long command with its wait, and arms a timer for a timed wait, since a lead waiting on outcomes alone read dead runs as working for hours (#528).
