- engineering-loop: the gatekeeper is `scripts/gatekeeper.py`, its GitHub calls behind three ports (tracker, forge, evidence) run by `scripts/github_adapter.py`, GitHub's adapter, which the policy's new `adapters` section picks per port (default `github`; a name no adapter has is refused, naming it). Its verbs are `check build|queue|merge`, `record approval|verdict|checks` (today's `approve`, `verdict` and `local-ci`), `land [--wait]`, `report [--json]`, `batch`, `delegate`, `brief`, `release` and `commit-msg`; `report --json <report>` prints a verifier report's facts as JSON. `scripts/approvals.py` stays for one release line as a shim that runs today's verbs unchanged and, imported, is the gatekeeper itself. The gate treats its own status as `loop:approvals` or `gatekeeper`, waiting for neither, and `land` and `check merge` refuse a PR's own copy of `github_adapter.py` as they refuse one of the gatekeeper (#614).
