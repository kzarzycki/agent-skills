- engineering: the package splits into `gatekeeper`, `loop`, `github`, `chief-of-staff` and `omnigent`, each a plugin of its own in the marketplace and an APM package of its own. `engineering` keeps the craft skills, `orchestrating-agents` with its native and herdr-link backends (its Omnigent backend is the `omnigent-agents` skill now) and a new `browsing` skill holding `browse.py`.
