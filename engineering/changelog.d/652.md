- The owner's go on a high-risk change is a commit status `owner` on its head: `approvals.py approve merge <pr> --by owner` posts it with a record whose every word the script writes, and the owner's own `approved:merge` on a PR into the default branch, added after the head's push, becomes that status. A push needs no label removed, since the status covers one commit; on a change that is not high risk the label means nothing (#652).
