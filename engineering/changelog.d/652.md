- The owner's go on a high-risk change is a commit status `owner` on its head. `approvals.py approve merge <pr> --head <sha> --by owner` posts it with a record whose every word the script writes, refusing a head other than the one the owner was shown; `--withdraw` posts a newer failing status, removes `approved:merge` and records it. The owner's own `approved:merge` on a PR into the default branch, added after the head's push, becomes that status, which holds while the label is on. A push needs no label removed, since the status covers one commit; on a change that is not high risk the label means nothing (#652).
