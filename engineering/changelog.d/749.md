- engineering-loop: `approvals.py land` stopped by SIGTERM or SIGHUP (a killed process group, a harness timeout) releases the landing lock on its way out and exits 128 plus the signal, so the next land no longer waits up to 2 hours for it to go stale. A stop that arrives while the lock is taken or released is covered too: from the release on, a stop waits until the lock is released, and a second stop (`uv run` forwards the signal Python already got) is ignored. A land started with SIGHUP ignored (under `nohup`) still outlives a hangup. SIGKILL still leaves the lock to be broken as stale (#749).
