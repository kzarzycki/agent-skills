- engineering-loop: the policy's `risk.merge_paths`, a list of globs beside `risk.paths`, holds the merge of any PR touching a matching path for the owner's own go on its head, whatever its labels, `spec.auto` and the verifier's `Risk:` line say, into any branch. `check merge`, `check queue` and `land` wait for that go and name the matching path, a new push needs a new go, and `approve merge --by delegate`, `delegate` and `approved:merge` give none there.
