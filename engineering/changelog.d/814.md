- engineering-loop: `approvals.py land` takes its landing lock on the branch `loop-landing/<base>`, which only moves forward, in place of the ref `refs/loop/landing`, since a cloud session's proxy refuses a push outside `refs/heads/` and every ref delete, so a cloud session could neither take nor release the old lock. The tip says the state: each lock commit holds the empty tree, is titled `landing lock: held` or `landing lock: free` with `[skip ci]`, and carries the pid and a random value (no hostname, since the branch is public). A land pushes a `held` child of a `free` tip, a release a `free` child of its own `held` commit, a stale lock (older than 2 hours) is broken by a `held` child of it, each leased on the tip it read, so of two lands from one tip one wins and nothing is deleted or rewritten. For one release, `land` waits (exit 3) while the old `refs/loop/landing` is on `origin`, reading it and never writing it, and passing over one older than 2 hours, so a host still on the previous version and this one never land at once (#814).
