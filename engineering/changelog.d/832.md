- engineering-loop: with the loop.md line `Cloud verifier: worker session`, the Codex verifier of a Claude-written PR runs in the kept cloud worker's VM, in its checkout and build. `scripts/cloud_verifier.py` holds it: `login` once on the host, `seed` keeps that login fresh and stores a copy without its refresh token in the cloud environment, and `verify` runs the pass at the PR's head in the VM and posts the report as a PR comment (#832).
