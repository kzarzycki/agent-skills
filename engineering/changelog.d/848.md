Per-host limits live in a `HOST_LIMITS` table with `OMNIGENT_MIN_AVAILABLE_MB_<HOST>` and `OMNIGENT_MAX_LOAD_<HOST>` overrides, and `capacity --host <name>` judges another host (#848).
