- orchestrating-agents: `supervise --console` keeps reviving a followed lead whose failure is a transient provider error (`authentication_failed`, a Cloudflare `Attention Required` page, any 5xx such as a cloud create's 503, `overloaded`), after a backoff of 2, 5, 10, 20 minutes and then every 20, (also a turn whose host went away: a reboot, a host restart, a lost runner, a cloud worker cut silent), and reports it to the chief of staff only once the next revive would fall more than 2 hours after the first failure; the chain is kept in the lead's state (`retrying`), so a restart keeps it. Any other failure is revived once and then reported, as before (#904).
