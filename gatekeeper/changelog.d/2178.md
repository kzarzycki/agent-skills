- gatekeeper: `land.batch_size` sets the Mergify queue's PRs per batch (1: one PR per entry), and `approvals.py queue-config` renders `.mergify.yml` from the pack's mergify.yml with it and `batch_wait_seconds`. The template merges a batch only on `check-success = queue-run`, a CI job that passes once the batch's test step ran its selection, so a path that skips that step merges nothing (#2178). On the local queue, `land` refuses a head with no pass record (`gate-record <head>` exit 1), naming the record and the `local-ci --record` that makes it; a break-glass PR is not held (#2178). `affected-tests --record` records a passing narrowed run as `local-ci --record` records one, pushed as its `loop-gate/` branch, and `gate-record <head> -- <narrowed command>` reads it; a failing run, or one that can't tell, records nothing (#2178).
