- gatekeeper: each `levels` entry takes `rerun` (default `false` for `low`, `true` for `high` and `critical`). With it on, `check merge` and `land` refuse a head whose only proof is the worker's record: they wait for an independent rerun of its selection on its tree, a `local-ci --record --rerun` record that an app pushed (a CI run) or that a verifier pass on the head names on a `Rerun: <commit>` line. `local-ci --record --rerun` reruns the selection reusing no pass and keys its record apart from the worker's `check rerun <pr>` says whether a head needs a rerun, and `--no-push` keeps the rerun's record as a local branch for a CI job without a write token; this repo's `rerun.yml` runs the selection with a read-only token and pushes the record from a second job that runs none of the PR's code (policy.md, Independent rerun) (#2240).
