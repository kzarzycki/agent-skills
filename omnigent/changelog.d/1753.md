- omnigent-agents: `supervise --console` acts on a `STILL WORKING` that nothing wakes or that hands work back (#1753). A wait on a process the harness does not track ("waiting on pid 4242", a pid file or a log, with no `--notify`, `LOOP_NOTIFY`, child, CI or `Waits:` line), or in a cloud session on a background subagent, gets one nudge (`LEAD_UNTRACKED`) saying why nothing wakes it and how to wait instead, then act if the lead ends that way again; it used to record the turn as a named wait and leave the lead idle until the no-progress deadline. A hand-back, a `Handback: #<n> <what>` line or prose such as `#12 READY` or `is yours`, goes to the session it notifies at once.
