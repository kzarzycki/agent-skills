## Report Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- [ ] Every statistic has a citation with date and source
- [ ] Source types are labeled (independent vs vendor-funded matters)
- [ ] Contradictions are explicitly noted, not hidden
- [ ] Gaps section is honest about what's missing
- [ ] Executive summary captures the most surprising/important findings
- [ ] Recency requirement was met (no stale data presented as current)
- [ ] Tables are used for quantitative data (easier to scan than prose)
- [ ] Report stands alone — a reader with no context can follow it
